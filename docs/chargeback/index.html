Cowork Credit Chargeback

Reads the Microsoft 365 admin center credit export plus your Entra user list, and turns raw consumption into a departmental bill you can defend in a budget meeting.

  • Three billing models priced side by side — pay-as-you-go, prepaid, hybrid, with the cheapest flagged
  • Prepaid pool allocation — split by usage, by headcount, or by budgeted allowance
  • Per-user limits and an over-limit watchlist — who needs their allowance adjusted
  • Row-level security — department heads see only their own line
  • FOCUS cost view — the chargeback in FinOps Open Cost & Usage Specification columns
