MATURITY JOURNEY

Left-to-right: from foundational telemetry to differentiated analytics

Use this sequence as your operating model: establish trusted baseline signals first, then progressively increase depth, customization, and business-value storytelling.

Stage 1 · Foundation

1 Admin Center

Foundational, out-of-the-box reporting on Copilot usage and credit consumption — the trusted baseline every rollout starts from.

  • Usage & adoption in 7-day, 30-day, and 180-day aggregates
  • Credit / consumption signals for Copilot and agents
  • admin.microsoft.com → Reports → Usage → Microsoft 365 Copilot
  • Roles: AI Administrator, Reports Reader, or Global Reader
  • Data latency typically ~72 hours

Stage 2 · Rich analysis

2 Viva Insights

Adds customization and flexibility with prebuilt templates and dashboards.

  • Copilot Dashboard: auto-enabled for managers/leaders; auto-scopes to their own teams
  • Analyst Workbench: deeper custom analysis — requires Insights Analyst role

Stage 3 · Agent analytics

3 Studio Analytics

Per-agent usage analytics for custom Copilot Studio agents via the Power Platform admin center.

  • Billed messages and consumption per agent
  • Session transcripts for review and export
  • Sessions, success rate, and active users
  • Roles: Power Platform Administrator

Stage 4 · Differentiation

4 Analytics Hub

Brings the capabilities of the first three surfaces together and combines them intelligently.

  • Unifies signals across Admin Center, Viva, and Studio
  • Answers enterprise questions not readily answered by the first three
  • Adds net-new analytics and operationalizes data extraction

How it extends the capabilities ↓

STAGE 4 · ANALYTICS HUB

How Analytics Hub extends the capabilities

Analytics Hub sits on top of the prebuilt, in-product surfaces above — it doesn't replace them. It stitches their signals together and answers the questions that fall between the tools.

  • Cross-estate synthesis: unifies signals across Admin Center, Viva Insights, Power Platform admin center, Purview, Entra, and GitHub into one analysis layer.
  • Answers the in-between questions: resolves customer-specific questions (superuser and champion identification, license right-sizing, cross-surface adoption) that no single prebuilt report answers on its own.
  • Data export + Fabric pipelines: provides extraction and Microsoft Fabric pipelines for large-scale data handling, unlimited history, and cross-source joins.
  • Foundations for advanced reporting: creates the foundation for advanced, custom enterprise reporting and ROI narratives ready for leadership.
  • Fast field iteration: rapidly folds real customer learnings back into reusable reports, apps, and data workflows.
