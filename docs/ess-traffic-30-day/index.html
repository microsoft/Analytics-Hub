What changed during the month

Views were consistent after the launch period: every day in September had traffic, and each of the last three week-buckets landed between 214 and 229 views.

Clones were front-loaded by a single Sep 6 spike of 173 clones from 5 unique cloners. Removing that one day, the repo still averaged about 6 clones per day for the rest of the month.

How to interpret it

ESS is not one of the highest-volume Hub reports yet, but the audience looks qualified. People are opening setup instructions and assets needed to deploy, not only the repository landing page.

Current top referrers are Teams, GitHub, LinkedIn, Google, ChatGPT, and Bing. Teams is the clearest distribution channel in the captured referrer list.
