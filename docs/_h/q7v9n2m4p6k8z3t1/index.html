Financial and timing assumptions

Rates are currency units per credit; budget is a horizon total. Current month 0 is before rollout. Checkpoints are comma-separated month numbers.

Phases

Starts must be strictly increasing, begin at month 1, and fall within the horizon. Growth is monthly, including month 1. A blank name becomes “Phase N”.

Departments

Counts may be fractional in forecasts. The prepaid allocation is shared, not repeated for each department.

Phase contribution waterfall

Final-month credits: initial monthly demand plus sequential phase contributions. Each contribution is the difference when that phase is added to the earlier phases; later phases are held at the preceding phase’s rates with no new cohort.

05 / Local data

Actuals & exports

Import a local CSV for visual comparison, or export the independent monthly forecasts.

CSV format: first row exactly month,credits,cost,active; one numeric row per month, with no extra columns. Month must be a unique integer from 1 to the alternative horizon; other values must be finite and nonnegative. Blank lines are ignored. No quoted values or formulas.

No actuals imported.

Model notes. Users and usage grow independently each month, with active users capped by enabled users. Newly enabled capacity is capped at eligible users and shared proportionally. Prepaid credits are allocated across departments in proportion to demand; only consumed prepaid is charged. Monthly allocations renew; a total pool is consumed over the horizon. PAYG covers overage. Budget is spread evenly only for monthly comparisons; budget breach is the first cumulative cost strictly above the horizon budget. Prepaid first exhaustion is the first month a positive allocation is fully consumed. Costs exclude taxes, fixed fees and contractual terms. Rates and usage are assumptions, not billing guidance.
