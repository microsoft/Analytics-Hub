Inside the app

What it shows

A few of its pages.

Adoption

Who started, who stayed and who stuck.

Value

The work Copilot helped with, the hours saved and what they're worth.

Consumption

The credits you used and what they cost.

Readiness

Who to license next, and who's ready for Cowork.

Agent Evaluation

How well your Copilot Studio agents answer, and where they fall short.

Governance

Who owns your agents, who can reach them, and what needs a review.

Plus an executive summary, leaderboards and more. The Consumption and Agent Evaluation pages need their add-ons. See every page in the app guide ↗

The installer can also publish the ValueLens, Consumption Central and Agent Evaluator Power BI reports, on the same models as the app. The Power BI reports ↗

Where it runs

Two ways to run it

Both load the data and publish the models.

On Microsoft Fabric

Runs in a Fabric workspace on your own capacity. Set up with the installer, or by hand.

Fabric set-up ↗

On Azure

Runs in your own Azure subscription, with the reports in a Power BI workspace. Set up with the installer, or by hand.

Azure set-up ↗
