Major change

Required minimum spending limits added — 1,000 credits per user

Microsoft’s Copilot Credit billing documentation now sets required minimum spending limits. The policy-level minimum is 1,000 credits per user (recommended 2,500) and the individual user monthly minimum is 2,000 credits (recommended 7,000). If a per-user limit is set below the required minimum, a warning is shown and the policy cannot be saved. Detected 10 Sep 2026.

 14 Sep 2026
Managing AI experiences enabled by usage-based billing

"Credits consumed before the move remain billed against the previous spending policy." Worked example: "the user is moved to Group 2… The user's usage is reset to 0 and now aligned to Spending Policy B."

"…the user's consumption history carries forward across the policy change. Credits consumed under the previous spending policy remain part of the user's consumption history and are accounted for when enforcing the spending limit under the new policy. Moving a user between groups or spending policies doesn't reset the user's consumption. Spending limits are preserved when users switch between billing policies."

New worked example: a user consumes 500 credits under Policy A, moves to Policy B, and "the user's 500 credits of prior consumption remain accounted for… up to the spending limit configured in Spending Policy B" — this "reduces the risk that a user could receive a new spending allocation by moving between departments, projects, groups, or spending policies."

A mid-month policy move no longer resets per-user usage to 0; consumption carries forward and the new policy's limit is enforced against it. Published date moved to 10 Sep 2026. Policy-level billing of pre-move credits is retained ("…retained for billing at a policy level but aren't tracked at a user level in the Consumption tab view"), and spending policies remain "limit-based controls [that] don't reserve or allocate" credits.

Managing AI experiences enabled by usage-based billing + billing overview Part of the same 10 Sep update (core page grew 4,204 → 5,376 words, +8,299 characters).
