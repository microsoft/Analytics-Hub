Which repos dropped, and which drops are real

GitHub repo views fell 44% on a typical weekday. This page shows which repos drove that, and separates a real loss of adoption from people simply downloading templates without opening the repo page.

Weekday averages, Sep 14 to Oct 8 compared with Oct 9 to Oct 19, 2026 · Source: GitHub Traffic API, 22 repos · Companion to the traffic diagnosis

Summary

Four repos explain two thirds of the drop. Only some of it is real.

Consumption Central alone accounts for 30% of lost views, and its downloads halved, so that is the one to act on. What I did with Cowork and PAX are also in real decline. AI-in-One, ValueLens and Cowork Chargeback lost page views but kept or grew their downloads, which means people are still getting the template.

How to read this page (hover or tap any underlined term for its definition)
Real declineBoth page views and downloads fell by 25% or more. Fewer people are actually getting this report, so this is a genuine loss of adoption.
Early warningDownloads fell 10 to 25%, or page views stayed flat while downloads dropped. Not a clear loss yet, but worth checking again in two weeks.
Downloads holdingFewer people opened the GitHub page, but the number downloading the report stayed the same or went up. People are still getting it, often straight from the Hub, so this is not lost adoption.
Views per weekdayHow many times the repo pages on GitHub were opened on an average Monday to Friday. Weekends are left out because they are always low.
DownloadsHow many different people (or machines) downloaded a copy of the repo per day, each counted once. This is our best measure of people actually taking the report.
Share of dropOf all the weekday views lost across every repo, the percentage that came from this one repo.

What to do next

RepoSuggested action
Consumption CentralHighest priority. Referrals from both the Hub and Teams fell by about a third. Feature it in the next session and refresh its Teams-ready link.
What I did with CoworkIt gets no traffic from the Hub, only from Teams, which fell. Add a stronger Hub entry point and re-share after the next Cowork session.
PAXIts traffic comes mainly from elsewhere on GitHub, so Hub changes do not explain this one. A clear v2 release note, shared in Teams, could re-engage users.
AI-in-One, ValueLens, Cowork ChargebackNo action on adoption. Report downloads alongside views so these do not look like losses.

Notes: "Downloads" are GitHub unique cloners: distinct people or systems downloading the repo each day, the best available measure of adoption. GitHub only lists each repo's top 10 pages, so a page vanishing from that list does not mean zero. Some ValueLens and What I did with Cowork page losses are renamed folders and new file versions. Oct 12 was a US holiday and sits in the recent window. Repos with fewer than 15 views per weekday are left out of the charts.
