Mockup notes

What the data actually supports

What changed from v7

  1. The tier label is gone, the tier filter is not. The row that read TIER beside a single Core only pill now renders the pill on its own. The 108px label gutter survives as an empty .mk-flabel--spacer so the pill keeps the same left edge as the Category and Focus area pills above it — without it the pill would slide into the gutter and the three rows would stop lining up. Under 720px, where .mk-flabel becomes flex-basis:100%, the spacer is display:none so it cannot cost a blank row. The button id, aria-pressed contract and filter behaviour are untouched.
  2. GitHub stars come from a second JSON island. #mk-stars carries the output of the daily sync_stars.py job; #mk-data is byte-identical to v7. 17 of the 22 reports resolve to a repository and show a count. The other five — roi-calc, cowork-chargeback-app, cowork-policy-helper-app, finops-focus-app and cowork-roi-model-app — are Pages apps, carry stars:null and render no star element at all, not a zero and not a dash. The count sits in the card foot next to the source line at source-line scale, because it is a property of the repository, not a measure of the report; it is deliberately kept out of the hero stat strip.
  3. Stale counts are marked, not hidden and not faked. A record with stale:true still renders its last known number — blanking it would throw away the only figure available — but it takes a dashed underline, cursor:help and a tooltip naming its fetchedAt date. The current payload has zero stale records, so this path is exercised by injection, not by the shipped data.
  4. Freshness is stated once, and derived. One quiet line at the foot of the filter panel reads fetchedAt off the payload. No cadence is asserted in the copy, because the copy cannot know whether the job actually ran.
  5. The flyout actions moved to opposite corners. Download template is now top right, in a flex row it shares with the close button, so the two are laid out rather than stacked and cannot overlap at any width. View on GitHub stays bottom left and is now a button.
  6. The GitHub button is the live .nav-cta, tokens and all. background:var(--mk-text) and color:var(--mk-bg), mapped from live’s --text and --bg. It is not a black chip: it renders near-black on near-white in light and inverts to near-white on near-black in dark. A hardcoded #000 would have looked right in light and disappeared in dark.

What changed from v6

  1. The invented background is gone. v5 painted three zones; v6 replaced them with one document-length blue gradient on .mk-page plus an SVG dither layer to stop it Mach-banding. Both are deleted. v7 takes the live homepage’s treatment instead, which is two rules and nothing else: body{ background:var(--bg) } and .band:nth-of-type(even){ background:var(--bg-alt) }. There is no linear-gradient anywhere on this page as a page or band background, in either theme.
  2. The two colours were measured, not copied out of the stylesheet. docs/index.html was loaded headless and computed style read off body and every .band. Light: ground #faf9f8, alternate #f7f7fb. Dark: #14141b and #14141d. Worth knowing: :nth-of-type counts <section> siblings, not .band siblings, so on live it is the 1st, 3rd and 5th band in document order that take --bg-alt, and .hero and .measurables-band re-assert --bg explicitly. The visible result is a clean alternation from the hero down, which is what is reproduced here.
  3. The mockup’s own tokens were corrected against live. The light ground was #ffffff and live computes #faf9f8 — the :root block near the foot of styles.css overrides the one at the top. Eight dark neutrals had drifted too and are now the live value verbatim: --bg-alt, --surface, --surface-2, --border, --border-strong, --text, --text-soft and --text-muted.
  4. Six re-cut values were deleted rather than kept. --mk-hero-lead, --mk-hero-eyebrow, --mk-link-on-wash, --mk-card-border, --mk-stat-border and --mk-pill-border existed only because the wash was too dark for the live values. On a near-white ground the live values clear their thresholds again, so each one reverts to the live token it was shadowing. The single exception is the “Clear filters” link, which uses --mk-accent-strong rather than --mk-accent: plain #0078d4 on the ground is 4.19:1, under the 4.5:1 AA needs for 13px text. That is a live-site value that does not pass, and it is called out rather than inherited silently.
  5. The sticky filter row got simpler, not more complicated. v6 needed a backdrop blur and a double-faded veil because a transparent sticky strip lets content scroll through it. The strip is now an opaque band colour at rest, so none of that is needed. The zero-height sentinel and the IntersectionObserver are unchanged — the sticky logic never depended on the wash — and .is-stuck now adds one small shadow and nothing else.
  6. Separation now comes from the cards. The two page colours are about one L* apart, so the alternation is deliberately almost invisible; that is exactly how the live homepage reads and it is the point. The group header is a --mk-surface block on the band, the cards are raised surfaces with the live --mk-border outline, and the only colour on the page comes from each card’s own --c. Nothing was tinted to compensate for the neutral ground.

What changed from v4

  1. The lede says where the reports came from. v4 explained how to use the page and nothing else. The reports are answers to gaps customers actually hit, and the page never said so. The opening sentence now carries that; the practical half — what the report looks like, what data it needs, who has to be in the room — is kept verbatim because it is the part that tells people what to do. Two alternates are in the decisions list below, unrendered, so the wording can be swapped without a rebuild.
  2. The three zones are genuinely different tones now. v4 set the hero to color-mix(accent 4%, --mk-bg-alt) and the filter band to color-mix(accent 3%, --mk-surface-2). Those are one percentage point of the same accent over two greys that differ by four sRGB steps, so the whole top of the page was one flat wash. v5 replaced both with explicit tokens: the hero got a real blue gradient (#c3dcf5 to #f2f7fd, 168°, no animation), the filter band drops to a flat cool neutral (#e3e6ee) that carries no chroma so it sits below the hero in weight, and the content area stays white. Measured lightness: hero stops L* 88.1 and 97.0, filter band L* 91.2, content L* 100. In v4 the hero and the filter band were 1.006:1 apart. Superseded twice: v6 replaced the three fills with one continuous wash, and v7 deletes the wash as well and takes the live homepage’s two-colour band alternation instead — see above.
  3. The stat cards were re-cut to survive the gradient. Their --mk-border outline measured 1.06:1 against the blue stop — they would have dissolved into it. They now carry --mk-stat-border, which clears 3:1 at both stops in both themes, plus --mk-shadow-sm to actually read as raised.
  4. Two knock-on fixes. The darker filter band pushed the second-row pill outline from 3.12:1 to 2.87:1 and the category-pill outline from 1.33:1 to 1.01:1, both under the 3:1 WCAG 1.4.11 wants of a control boundary. Both pill families shared --mk-pill-border — 3.19:1 in light, 4.75:1 in dark. The category pills were the one component v4 reproduced verbatim from docs/styles.css, so that was the single place v5 stopped matching live; it was written up as decision 5 with a one-line revert rather than absorbed silently. Superseded in v7: with the band fills gone both pill families are back on the live --mk-border, and the override is deleted.

What changed from v3

  1. Flyout media now shows video and image together. Where a report has both an embeddable demo video and a preview image, the flyout offers two tabs and opens on the video. Where it has a video and no image, the video is embedded instead of the “no preview” placeholder — that is the Consumption Dashboard fault. Only one media element is in the DOM at a time; the video is controls preload="metadata", never autoplay, never looped.
  2. A corrected title, applied at render time. See the live-data bugs below.
  3. The hero and the filter block have structure. The masthead sits on a tinted band, the four counts are stat blocks rather than body text, and the category, focus-area, tier and result rows are now one bordered control surface instead of loose pills on white.
  4. The group headers assert themselves. Accent left border, accent wash, a heavier 1.625rem title and the count as a real badge, with 76px of air above each header and 16px below it so the header owns the cards under it.

Live-data bugs this mockup does not hide

All 64 outbound URLs carried by the dataset were requested before this build. 63 resolve. One does not.

  1. Broken download — “What Cowork Did For Me” (cowork-impact). Its download URL, …/What-I-did-with-Cowork/raw/main/cowork-roi-report-skill-v24.zip, returns 404. This is a live data bug, not a mockup bug, and it is still outstanding. The flyout still renders the Download button, because that is what the live data says it should render.
  2. Wrong title — cowork-billing-report. The live dataset says “Copilot Cowork Billing Report”. It should say “Cowork Chargeback Report Power BI”. This is an OUTSTANDING live-data fix in docs/explore-reports/app.js — it has not been corrected at source. This mockup renders the corrected string through a small, labelled PENDING_DATA_FIXES map so the embedded JSON stays a character-faithful copy of live and stays verifiable against it.
  3. The /archive/refs/heads/*.zip links redirect to codeload.github.com. That is normal GitHub behaviour and was not counted as a fault.

    What changed from v2

    v2 removed all colour and read as flat. v3 restores the homepage rf-card system: each card sets --c inline from its own accent value, and that colour drives three things only — the 11%-tinted category chip, the “View” text, and the hover border. Nothing else is coloured. The per-report icon field is still never rendered anywhere on this page.

    Because every card now carries a coloured chip naming its category, v2’s small uppercase category eyebrow on each card was duplicate information and has been removed. The chip replaces it.

    v2’s sticky jump-nav and these category pills both navigate the same axis, so keeping both would have been two competing controls for one job. The jump-nav is gone; the pills stay, because that is what the live homepage uses. Focus area and Core only survive as a quieter second row.

    Accent palette in use, derived at load time:

    Known legibility issue, reproduced not fixed: one accent is #24292f (GitHub Copilot Impact). At 11% tint over a dark card it is almost invisible and the chip text is near-black on near-black. This is exactly how it behaves on the live homepage today. It has been left alone here rather than silently corrected, so you can decide whether to change the source value or add a dark-theme floor.

    The vote affordance is fake here

    The thumbs-up and the 0 beside it are a static, non-interactive <span>, present only so the card matches the live screenshot. It does not click, it is hidden from screen readers, and the 0 is a placeholder, not a real count. Live counts come from docs/votes.js, which is not loaded in this mockup.

    Decisions needed from you

    1. Which lede? The hero currently renders the primary. The two alternates below are parked here verbatim and are not rendered anywhere on the page; say the word and either one swaps straight in.
      • Rendered now: “None of these started on a whiteboard. Every report here traces back to a gap a customer actually hit — a question they needed answered and had no way to answer. Start with yours: see what the report looks like, what data it needs and who has to be in the room, then take the template.”
      • Alternate A: “Every report here answers a question a customer actually asked. Nothing on this page was invented in a vacuum — each one closed a real gap for a real team. Start with the question you need answered, then take the template.”
      • Alternate B: “These reports exist because someone needed them. Each one began as a customer problem with no good answer, and became the report that answered it. Start with the question you need answered, then take the template.”
      Note that both alternates drop “what data it needs and who has to be in the room”, which is the only place the page tells you what a report will cost you to run.
    2. Cards lead with blurb now, as on the homepage. Do you want the grid to keep scanning on question instead, as v2 did?
    3. The flyout is media-led, but even after v4’s video fix eight of the 22 reports still open to the typographic placeholder, and two whole groups (Readiness, Tooling & Extension) have no media of any kind. Record previews for the gaps, accept the typographic placeholder as the permanent state, or let those reports open to a text-first layout with no media slot?
    4. When a report has both, v4 opens on the video. Is that the right default, or should the GIF/still lead because it paints immediately while the mp4 needs a click?
    5. The category pills are reproduced from docs/styles.css, which gives them a --mk-border outline. v5 and v6 overrode it with --mk-pill-border so it would survive their invented band fills. v7 reverts to the live value, because matching live was the instruction and the override was only ever there to prop up a background that no longer exists. The consequence is that the pill outline is a faint boundary again — the same faint boundary it is on the live homepage today, and below the 3:1 WCAG 1.4.11 asks of a control boundary. The pills are still identifiable by their fill, their label and their count badge, and the selected pill is a solid accent lozenge. Accept the live outline as-is, fix --border on the live site so every bordered control benefits, or reinstate a mockup-only override here?
    6. Fix the two live-data bugs at source in docs/explore-reports/app.js — the 404 download on cowork-impact and the cowork-billing-report title — so the PENDING_DATA_FIXES map can be deleted?
    7. Flyout size: keep 70% as the default, or drop to 50%? Toggle both in the control bar above.
    8. Keep related derived automatically from shared tags, or author three related reports per report by hand?
    9. Second filter row (focus area, core only): keep it, or are the category pills enough on their own now that the jump-nav is gone?
    10. Fix #24292f for dark mode, or leave it as it is on the live site?
    11. The current live page has an inline row-expand and a separate lightbox. Do both retire in favour of this single flyout?
